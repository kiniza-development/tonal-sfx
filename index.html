What are tonal sound effects?

Tonal SFX have a definite musical pitch and are tuned to a key such as C major or A minor. Whooshes, footsteps and doors are noise-based and have no pitch. Because tonal SFX share a key with your music, you can stack them under a licensed track, or on each other, without clashing. Editors use them like Lego to build a custom score on the timeline.

  • FoundationDrones, pads, pedals and textures that set mood and weight.
  • MidRhythmic loops, arps and chord progressions for pulse and harmony.
  • TopMelodies, dialogue phrases and signature lines for the focal moment.
  • GlueRisers, pitched impacts, brand stings and transitions to carry the cuts.

Every app has a key picker, a reproducible seed with Today and Dice, a waveform and piano roll, and WAV export including a 12-key pack. The URL hash stores the whole pattern, so you can bookmark it or share it. Click once before audio starts, because browsers block autoplay.
